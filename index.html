Rule Book: How to Play Pasha Palli

ORIGIN & CULTURAL HERITAGE

The Ancient Epic Dice Race

From "An Anthology of Native & Indigenous Sports of India" (CUTM, 2025):

"Pasha Palli is an ancient Indian indigenous board game, primarily a game of chance and strategy, played with dice and pawns on a cruciform board. It is widely recognized as the historic precursor to modern games like Ludo and Pachisi. Mentions of the game trace back thousands of years to ancient Indian texts, including the epic Mahabharata, where a game of dice (Pasha) plays a pivotal role."

In Odisha and across the subcontinent, Pasha Palli represents the quintessential dice-pit cross race. It is played on embroidered velvet cloth boards with deep crimson and emerald checked arms.

BOARD GEOMETRY & TRAVERSAL

Cruciform Board & Counter-Clockwise Track

  • Setup: Each player controls 4 Gottis starting in their designated corner base dock.
  • Entering Play: A roll of 1, 6, or Doubles releases a Gotti from the starting dock onto step 0 of your home arm.
  • Traversal Path: Gottis travel down the center column, loop counter-clockwise around the outer perimeter of all four arms, and finally travel up the center home column into the central Charkoni 'C'.
  • Exact Roll to Finish: A Gotti must reach the central 'C' by an exact count. Overshooting is invalid.
TACTICS & DEFENSE

Captures, Safe Castles & Defensive Pairs

  • Capturing: Landing on an opponent's single pawn sends it back to its starting base dock and immediately awards you an Extra Turn!
  • Safe Castle Havens (X): Cells marked with stitched golden 'X' marks are fortified castles. Pawns here are immune to capture and multiple players can safely share the tile.
  • Defensive Pairs / Blocks: When two of your pawns occupy the same non-safe square, they form an impenetrable Pair Block. Opponents cannot capture them and cannot pass over them!
  • Doubles: Rolling matching faces on the Pasas awards a bonus throw.
VICTORY CONDITIONS

Winning the Match & Ranked Finishes

The first player to guide all 4 of their Gottis into the central Charkoni 'C' wins the match!

In 3 and 4-player matches, play continues to determine 2nd, 3rd, and 4th place ranks.

INPUT & CONTROLS (§8 SPECIFICATION)

Mouse & Keyboard Controls (Dual Support)

  • 🖱️ Mouse: Click the THROW PASAS button or the dice tray directly to roll. Click any glowing Gotti to preview its path and destination. Click the highlighted target cell, click the Gotti again, or click Confirm Move to advance. Click outside to deselect.
  • ⌨️ Keyboard:
    • Space or Enter: Throw Pasas / Confirm selected move
    • 1, 2, 3, 4: Select Gotti #1 through #4 directly
    • Tab / Arrow Keys / WASD: Cycle between eligible Gottis
    • Esc: Cancel move preview or Pause game
    • Backspace: Cancel preview or Undo last move
    • H: Strategic AI Hint | U: Casual Move Undo
    • M: Toggle Audio Mute | R: Reset Board
